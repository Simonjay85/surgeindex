# Ranking leagues

Leagues reduce unfair comparisons between a new site and a mature site with a long, stable history.

- **New**: early observation window or insufficient scale/history for an established comparison.
- **Emerging**: enough accepted history to compare momentum, but not an established traffic base.
- **Established**: sustained history and volume support a mature comparison set.

League assignment is derived from completed data days, accepted 7-day volume, baseline evidence, and the previous league. Hysteresis keeps a site from bouncing on one update; a downgrade requires the lower condition to persist. League membership changes the comparison set, not the underlying Heat formula.

Scopes include global, global-by-league, category, and category-by-league. Paid placements never move a site between leagues.
